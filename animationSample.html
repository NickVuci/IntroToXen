Interactive lesson

The harmonic series

First, watch simple frequency ratios become visible as waveforms. Then explore the harmonic series directly by clicking partials, hearing them, and combining them into colon-notated harmonic sets.

What to notice: 2/1 fits two upper periods into one lower period. 3/1 fits three. 4/1 fits four. Higher harmonic numbers mean more cycles in the same amount of time.

Part 2

Click the harmonic series

Click a partial to start it. Click it again to stop it. Multiple partials can sound together; when two or more are active, they appear as colon notation such as 4:5:6.

How to read it: partial 1 is the fundamental. Partial 2 is twice the frequency, partial 3 is three times the frequency, and so on. A single partial is shown as n/1. Multiple sounding partials are notated as a colon-separated harmonic set.
