Figures

Each elaboration level unlocks more figures: Light uses the first three, Ornate adds four more, Florid adds the tirata and groppo. Untick a figure to keep it out of the piece. Every figure is checked against the lower voice before it is used: dissonant notes must pass or neighbour by step and stay off beats 1 and 3, and no figure may create parallel perfects. In sequences one figure is chosen per model slot and repeated in every unit.

Bass diminution in sequences, schemata and the medial cadence

Trommelbass repeats the bass note in eighths; murky bass alternates it with its lower octave; walking bass moves in quarters by step toward the next bass note. Each is checked against the upper voice for parallels before it is used.

Schemata

Galant schemata as two-voice skeletons, one event per half bar, realized in this scale and tuning. Each is built from steps and two anchors found above the chosen root: the degree nearest 4/3 and the degree nearest 3/2. Leading tones are raised by a chroma where the step below a root is large.

Four-event stepwise patterns that stay consonant throughout, ranked by imperfect consonances, contrary motion and how many scale degrees they can start on. A pattern that starts on every degree is ready to use as a sequence model.

Harmony

Each bass note implies a chord, read as a figured bass. Intervals are sorted into thirds, fourths, fifths, sixths and sevenths by their nearest just ratio, not by how many scale steps they span. A key is a root in one of the two standard modes; they differ only in the degree below the root, so changing mode raises or lowers that degree by a chroma.

Save and transfer

A save holds the tuning (step sizes, degree cents, generator, EDO mapping when the hardness is a simple ratio), every note of both voices with its degree, cents, frequency and timing, and the settings needed to open the piece here again exactly as it is.

Audio and MIDI

Renders with the current sound, tempo and tonic. MP4 and WebM save as a single ordinary audio file that plays anywhere (an audio-only .mp4 is the same thing as an .m4a). The viewer does not allow saving .wav, .mp3 or .mid files directly, so those come inside a .zip; the MIDI zip also holds a Scala tuning file and the JSON save. With separate voices, MP4 and WebM save each voice as its own file.
